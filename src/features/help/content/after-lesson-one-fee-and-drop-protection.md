Lesson 1 is the match delivered; Lesson 2 is the match kept. This article walks through what happens after that first session — how the fee lands, what Drop-Protection does if the family stops after one paid lesson, and what we do on your side when things go wrong.

## The goal is Lesson 2

Most families who stop, stop between Lesson 1 and Lesson 2. Nothing dramatic happens — timetables shift, a grandparent objects, the term gets busy. The strongest thing you can do for retention is end Lesson 1 with a concrete next step: agree the Lesson 2 date and time before you leave the chat, and send a short recap of what you covered plus what comes next. [Your first WhatsApp intro and lesson ground rules](/help/parent-communication/introduce-yourself-whatsapp) covers how we open that conversation.

Not sure the family is fully sold? You can offer a free 15–20 minute online fit call before Lesson 1 — a short video call to discuss goals and expectations. It is a conversation, not a free full lesson, and it filters out the matches least likely to continue.

## When the administrative matching fee lands

The fee is 100% of Lesson 1 + 50% of Lesson 2, finished by Lesson 2 — the full breakdown and the centre ladder live in [The 1.5-lesson admin fee and unlocking parent contact](/help/payments-policies/admin-fee-structure). The timing in practice:

1. **Lesson 1 completed and reported** — the Lesson 1 portion (100%) is accrued. It is owed even if the case stops before Lesson 2, because the match was delivered.
2. **Lesson 2 completed** — the remaining 50% accrues and the fee is invoiced in full.
3. **You settle by FPS** — parent contact stays open and later lessons carry no further platform charge.

Report lessons promptly and accurately. Your reports feed your verified teaching hours, which drive your Blue Tick signals and your placement in matching — early drops and honest reports both count as real history.

## Drop-Protection: if the family stops after one paid lesson

If a parent or centre stops after exactly one paid lesson and MatchMax verifies the stop with them, you do not owe the full Lesson 1 portion. Verified stop → the fee for that case is 0.5 lessons: you keep the other half of the Lesson 1 tuition you were paid.

How it works:

- **Verification is ours, not yours.** The team confirms the stop reason with the parent or centre once. You never have to chase proof, and your word alone never reduces the fee — the same rule protects you in both directions.
- **Tutor-fault stops are not protected.** If the stop traces to something on your side — no-shows, unprepared lessons, conduct — protection is void and the full fee stands.
- **A verified stop still counts as real history.** It feeds your verified hours and completion signals like any other case.
- **If a parent is hostile or abusive,** tell the team with evidence. In verified cases you keep the same 0.5-lesson treatment and the household is not re-matched to you. You are an Independent Tutor running your own practice; we handle the household side.

## If you are the one who has to stop

Tell the team as early as you can. If you have a genuine emergency, we can stop without penalty to your standing. Otherwise, stopping after Lesson 1 affects your reliability signals and your access to new first lessons — and the family gets a priority rematch either way, so the sooner we know, the better the outcome for everyone, including you.

## After Lesson 1, in one checklist

1. Agree Lesson 2 before you close the chat.
2. Report Lesson 1 to MatchMax Admin.
3. Send a two-line recap: what you covered, what comes next.
4. If anything feels off with the family, say so early — the team can step in between lessons.
5. Report Lesson 2 and settle the invoice when it arrives.
