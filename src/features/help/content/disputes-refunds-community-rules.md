# Disputes, Refunds & Community Rules

These answers follow the [Notes Terms](/legal/notes-terms). If anything here differs from the Notes Terms, the Notes Terms apply.

## For buyers

**When can I get a refund?**
Before staff confirm your FPS payment, they simply reject it and you are never charged. After the file is released to you, the seller's share is held for **48 hours** (72 hours for new sellers) or until you tap "I received it, no issue", whichever comes first. A valid dispute raised during that hold is refunded from the held funds.

**What counts as a valid dispute?**
A file that is defective or corrupt, materially different from its preview, or not as described. Changing your mind, or a grade you hoped for, is not a valid dispute: notes are study aids and no file promises a result.

**How do I open a dispute?**
Open the order in your account, choose **Open a dispute** during the hold window, pick the reason, and add a short description and a screenshot if you have one.

**What happens after the hold ends?**
Once the hold ends and the seller has been paid, there is no refund promise. MatchMax never pays a notes refund out of its own money.

## How staff decide

Staff check three points on every dispute:

1. **Download log checked:** whether and when the file was opened.
2. **Master file matches the preview:** the delivered file is the one shown on the listing.
3. **No banned content:** nothing from the ban list on the notes guide.

If any point fails, it is the seller's fault and the buyer is refunded in full from the hold. If all three pass, the dispute is usually rejected and the hold is released as normal. Staff can also refund part of the price where only part of the file is affected.

## For sellers: community rules

- Upload only your own work, or material you have written permission to sell.
- No exam-board papers, marking schemes, school-internal material, or anything else on the ban list.
- No phone numbers, payment handles, or other contact details inside files or listings.
- The file buyers receive must match its preview and description.

**Strikes.** A seller-fault dispute can add a strike to your account. Strikes stay on your account even if your account is suspended for a while and then restored. Repeated breaches close your notes-selling privilege; serious breaches suspend selling altogether.

# 爭議、退款及社群守則

以下解答以[筆記條款](/legal/notes-terms)為準。如有任何分歧，概以筆記條款為準。

## 買家須知

**甚麼情況可以退款？**
職員確認你的轉數快付款之前，只會拒絕該筆付款，你不會被收費。檔案發放給你之後，賣家的款項會暫存 **48 小時**（新賣家為 72 小時），或直至你按下「我已收到，沒有問題」，以較早者為準。在暫存期內提出而成立的爭議，會從暫存款項中退款。

**甚麼屬於成立的爭議？**
檔案損壞或無法開啟、與預覽有重大分別，或與描述不符。改變主意，或未達到期望的成績，均不屬成立的爭議：筆記是學習輔助材料，任何檔案都不保證成績。

**如何提出爭議？**
在暫存期內，於帳戶中打開該訂單，選擇**提出爭議**，揀選原因，並寫下簡短描述；如有截圖，請一併附上。

**暫存期完結後會怎樣？**
暫存期完結並已向賣家付款後，便不再承諾退款。MatchMax 從不以自己的資金支付筆記退款。

## 職員如何處理

職員會就每宗爭議檢核三項：

1. **已檢查下載紀錄：**檔案是否及何時被打開。
2. **正本檔案與預覽相符：**交付的檔案與刊登時展示的相同。
3. **沒有違禁內容：**不含筆記指南所列的違禁內容。

任何一項不通過，即屬賣家過失，買家會從暫存款項獲全額退款。三項全部通過的話，爭議一般會被拒絕，暫存款項如常發放。如只有部分檔案受影響，職員亦可退回部分款項。

## 賣家須知：社群守則

- 只上載你自己的作品，或已取得書面許可出售的材料。
- 不可上載考評局或考試局試卷、評分準則、學校內部材料，或任何違禁清單上的內容。
- 檔案或刊登內容中不可包含電話號碼、收款帳號或其他聯絡資料。
- 買家收到的檔案必須與預覽及描述相符。

**違規紀錄。**屬賣家過失的爭議可能令你的帳戶增加一次違規紀錄。即使帳戶曾被暫停後再恢復，違規紀錄仍會保留。多次違規會令你失去出售筆記的權限；嚴重違規會被全面暫停出售。
