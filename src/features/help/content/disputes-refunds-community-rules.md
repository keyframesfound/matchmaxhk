# Disputes, Refunds & Community Rules

These answers follow the [Notes Terms](/legal/notes-terms). If anything here differs from the Notes Terms, the Notes Terms apply.

## For buyers

**When can I get a refund?**
You pay exactly the listed price by card, Apple Pay, AlipayHK or WeChat Pay, and no gateway surcharge is added on top. The file unlocks as soon as payment goes through, with no staff release step. MatchMax holds the seller's payout for **72 hours from the moment of payment**. A dispute raised inside those 72 hours and upheld on an objective ground is refunded from the held funds. There is no cooling-off period: changing your mind is not a ground.

**What counts as a valid dispute?**
Only grounds D1 to D9 of the [Notes Terms](/legal/notes-terms): a file that is missing or unreadable, corrupted or password-locked, materially different from its preview or description, or otherwise covered by those grounds. Changing your mind, or a grade you hoped for, is not a valid dispute: notes are study aids and no file promises a result.

**How do I open a dispute?**
Open the order in your account, choose **Open a dispute** within the 72 hours after payment, pick the reason, and add a short description and a screenshot if you have one.

**What happens after the 72 hours?**
Once the 72 hours pass and the seller is paid, the sale is final and there is no refund promise. Refunds come only from funds still held: MatchMax never pays a notes refund out of its own money.

## How staff decide

The seller has 48 hours to respond or send a repaired file once a dispute is open. Staff then check three points:

1. **Download log checked:** whether and when the file was opened.
2. **Master file matches the preview:** the delivered file is the one shown on the listing.
3. **No banned content:** nothing from the ban list on the notes guide.

If any point fails, it is the seller's fault and the buyer is refunded in full from the hold. If all three pass, the dispute is usually rejected and the payout is released as normal when the 72 hours end. Staff can also refund part of the price where only part of the file is affected.

## For sellers: community rules

**What I keep, and what MatchMax takes.** Buyers pay exactly your listed price. MatchMax charges a flat 2% platform fee on the gross price of every paid file, and the actual card processing fee (Stripe) is also deducted from your side, so the amount you receive is the listed price less 2% and less that processing fee. The 2% is a launch rate: it holds for the first 6 months after in-app checkout launched, or until cumulative study-notes sales reach HK$50,000, whichever comes first. Any change after that is announced 30 days in advance.

**When I get paid.** Your payout is released after the 72-hour buyer-protection window ends, then paid in the weekly Monday FPS batch (Sunday 23:59 HKT cut-off, minimum HK$50). A held or disputed order stays out of the batch until it is resolved.

- Upload only your own work, or material you have written permission to sell.
- No exam-board papers, marking schemes, school-internal material, or anything else on the ban list.
- No phone numbers, payment handles, or other contact details inside files or listings.
- The file buyers receive must match its preview and description.

**Strikes.** A seller-fault dispute can add a strike to your account. Strikes stay on your account even if your account is suspended for a while and then restored. Repeated breaches close your notes-selling privilege; serious breaches suspend selling altogether.

# 爭議、退款及社群守則

以下解答以[筆記條款](/legal/notes-terms)為準。如有任何分歧，概以筆記條款為準。

## 買家須知

**甚麼情況可以退款？**
你只需以信用卡、Apple Pay、支付寶香港或微信支付繳付標價，平台不會向買家加收任何付款手續費。付款完成後，檔案即時解鎖，毋須經職員發放。MatchMax 會由**付款一刻起計 72 小時**暫存賣家的款項。在這 72 小時內提出而成立的爭議，會從暫存款項中退款。本平台不設冷靜期，改變主意並非退款理由。

**甚麼屬於成立的爭議？**
只限[筆記條款](/legal/notes-terms) D1 至 D9 所列的理由：檔案缺失或無法下載、損壞或設有密碼、與預覽或描述有重大分別，或其他符合該等理由的情況。改變主意，或未達到期望的成績，均不屬成立的爭議：筆記是學習輔助材料，任何檔案都不保證成績。

**如何提出爭議？**
在付款後 72 小時內，於帳戶中打開該訂單，選擇**提出爭議**，揀選原因，並寫下簡短描述；如有截圖，請一併附上。

**72 小時過後會怎樣？**
72 小時屆滿並已向賣家付款後，交易即告最終，不再承諾退款。退款只會來自仍然暫存的款項，MatchMax 從不以自己的資金支付筆記退款。

## 職員如何處理

你提出爭議後，賣家有 48 小時回覆或提交修復檔案。職員隨後會檢核三項：

1. **已檢查下載紀錄：**檔案是否及何時被打開。
2. **正本檔案與預覽相符：**交付的檔案與刊登時展示的相同。
3. **沒有違禁內容：**不含筆記指南所列的違禁內容。

任何一項不通過，即屬賣家過失，買家會從暫存款項獲全額退款。三項全部通過的話，爭議一般會被拒絕，暫存款項於 72 小時屆滿時如常發放。如只有部分檔案受影響，職員亦可退回部分款項。

## 賣家須知：社群守則

**你會收到多少，平台收取多少。**買家只需支付你的標價。MatchMax 就每份收費教材的標價總額收取 2% 平台服務費，實際信用卡手續費（Stripe）亦會從你一方扣除，因此你收到的金額為標價減 2%，再減該筆付款手續費。2% 為推出優惠費率：由應用程式內信用卡結帳推出起計 6 個月內有效，或直至教材累計銷售額達 HK$50,000，以較早者為準。其後如有調整，會於 30 天前通知。

**何時出糧。**72 小時買家保障期屆滿後，款項會於每週一的轉數快批次發放（星期日 23:59 香港時間截數，最低 HK$50）。暫存或爭議中的訂單不會計入該批次，直至處理完畢。

- 只上載你自己的作品，或已取得書面許可出售的材料。
- 不可上載考評局或考試局試卷、評分準則、學校內部材料，或任何違禁清單上的內容。
- 檔案或刊登內容中不可包含電話號碼、收款帳號或其他聯絡資料。
- 買家收到的檔案必須與預覽及描述相符。

**違規紀錄。**屬賣家過失的爭議可能令你的帳戶增加一次違規紀錄。即使帳戶曾被暫停後再恢復，違規紀錄仍會保留。多次違規會令你失去出售筆記的權限；嚴重違規會被全面暫停出售。
