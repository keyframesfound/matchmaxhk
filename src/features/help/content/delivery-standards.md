# Setting Up for Success: Delivery Standards, Logistics, and Admin

## The MatchMax Service Standard

**Reliable. Clear. Prepared. Protective. Progress-focused.**

Elite tutoring begins before academic content. Strong tutors signal trust through punctuality, preparation, professional boundaries, clean communication, and a structured lesson flow. Every tutor represents MatchMax and shapes our reputation, so your success is our success—and our success is yours.

## Tutor Mindset: Weak Delivery vs. MatchMax Standard

| Weak delivery | MatchMax standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| Arrives exactly on time or late | Arrives 5-7 minutes early |
| Starts with textbook immediately | Starts with rapport and a goal check |
| Explains for long stretches | Uses short explanations and active student work |
| Gives vague praise | Reports specific progress and bottlenecks |
| Works in private or unclear spaces | Uses open, visible, appropriate learning environments |
| Completes work for the student | Teaches process, structure, and independent thinking |

## Delivery-Mode Standards

| Delivery mode | Required standard | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Face-to-face / in-home | Arrive 5-7 minutes early | Absorbs minor delays and signals reliability |
| Face-to-face / in-home | Dress smart casual | Creates a professional first impression |
| Face-to-face / in-home | Conduct lessons in visible, communal spaces such as a dining table or open study area | Supports boundaries, comfort, and safeguarding |
| Face-to-face / in-home | Sit at a 90-degree angle to the student | Feels collaborative without being intrusive. Sitting 90 degrees removes the confrontational feeling of sitting directly opposite them, while avoiding the boundary intrusion of sitting shoulder-to-shoulder. |
| Online / Zoom / Meet | Avoid talking over a static PDF for more than 3 minutes | Maintains student attention |
| Online / Zoom / Meet | Keep the screen active with highlighting, typing, drawing, questioning, or worked examples | Shows thinking clearly and resets focus. Students disengage rapidly online. |
| Online / Zoom / Meet | Use reliable internet, ideally hardwired | Reduces lesson disruption |
| Online / Zoom / Meet | Use dual monitors where available | Separates student view, lesson content, notes, and resources |
| Online / Zoom / Meet | Use a drawing tablet when helpful | Makes live annotation faster and clearer |
| Online / Zoom / Meet | Use good lighting and clear audio | Builds trust and reduces fatigue |

## Logistics & The "Always-A-Step-Ahead" Mindset

Never assume a parent remembers the schedule. If they forget, you waste your time. Use these scripts to proactively manage your schedule:

```copy
# The Day-Before Gentle Reminder
Hi [Parent], gentle reminder that [Student] has a lesson tomorrow at [Time]. I've already uploaded tomorrow's exercises to our shared Google Drive folder so they can take a look! 🫡
```

```copy
# The 2-Hours-Before Reminder
Hi! See you and [Student] online in a few hours at [Time]. Please have [Specific Material] ready. 🙏🏻
```

**Prepare and Organise:**

- Confirm the date, time, duration, location/link, and required materials.
- Finish preparation and equipment checks 10 minutes before the start. Be ready online a few minutes early; arrive at in-person locations exactly at the agreed time.
- Use a consistent Zoom link for each student/group and pin it in the chat so it's never lost.
- Maintain a separate Google Drive folder for each student containing notes, resources, homework, and the Study Plan. Pin the folder link in the chat and restrict access to authorised people.

## MatchMax Admin: Reporting Your Hours

While you manage the parent relationship, MatchMax manages the backend. To ensure billing is accurate and our commission is logged correctly, you are required to keep the MatchMax Admin updated during the crucial first two lessons.

```copy
# Confirming Lesson 1 Date (Send to Admin)
Hi Admin, Lesson 1 for [Student Name] is confirmed for [Date] at [Time] for [Duration]. 🫡
```

```copy
# Reporting Completed Lesson 1 (Send to Admin)
Hi Admin, Lesson 1 with [Student Name] completed successfully for [Number] hours! We are scheduling Lesson 2 now. 🙏🏻
```

**Finalizing the Schedule:** Once the regular weekly slot is locked in after the first couple of lessons, inform Admin of the recurring schedule so we can finalize the file.

## The Ultimate Session Checklist

**Before Session:**

- Time, location, and mode confirmed
- Goal and previous notes reviewed
- Diagnostic questions prepared
- Materials/links ready
- Smart casual dress met
- Arrival/login completed early
- Equipment checked
- Safeguarding setup checked

**During Session:**

- Rapport established
- Goal stated in plain language
- Retrieval warm-up completed
- Modeling kept concise
- Guided practice used
- Independent attempt completed
- Exact bottleneck identified
- Online lesson kept interactive

**After Session:**

- Next steps assigned
- Diagnostic notes recorded
- Parent update sent using Rule of 3
- Materials uploaded to Drive folder
- Next session confirmed
- Admin notified of hours
