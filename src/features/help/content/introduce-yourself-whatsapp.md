The first message you send after a match sets the tone for the whole engagement. A warm, specific introduction reassures the parent they made a good choice and gets scheduling settled before Lesson 1. This guide gives you a copy-paste script and the ground rules that keep the relationship professional.

## What to send in the first message

Keep it short: a greeting, who you are, and one or two scheduling questions. A strong first message includes:

- A warm greeting addressed to the parent (and the student, if the chat includes them).
- Your name and the subject or curriculum you specialise in, so the parent immediately connects you to their request.
- One or two concrete questions: what the student is currently working on, and which weekly slot works best.

Resist the urge to list every qualification you have. The match is already made; your job now is to be easy to talk to and easy to schedule.

## Copy-paste intro script

```script
# First WhatsApp intro
--en
Hello [Parent Name] and [Student Name]! I'm [Your Name], your matched [Subject] tutor. I specialize in [Curriculum] and my goal is to make sure [Student] feels confident going into their exams. Two quick things before Lesson 1: could you share what topics [Student] is working on, and which weekly slot works best for you?
--zh
[家長稱謂]、[同學名] 你哋好！我係 [導師名]，你哋配對嘅 [科目] 導師。我專注 [課程]，目標係令 [同學名] 對考試更有信心。上第一堂之前想確認兩件事：可唔可以話我知 [同學名] 而家讀緊嘅課題，同埋邊個每週時段最方便？
```

If the parent prefers a quick call first, keep it to about five minutes: greet them, confirm the subject and level, and agree the first slot. Save the deeper conversation about goals for the 15-minute study-plan chat after Lesson 1.

## Tone and reply expectations

- Be polite, clear, warm, and professional. Light emojis are fine in casual chats about scheduling; skip them in sensitive or billing conversations.
- Aim to reply promptly during the day. Parents remember tutors who answer quickly.
- Keep non-urgent messages to sensible daytime hours, and expect the same in return.
- Do not treat an unconfirmed booking as agreed. A time is only locked once the parent has explicitly confirmed it. See [Confirm lesson dates, reschedules, and tutor sick days](/help/scheduling-logistics/coordinate-lesson-dates) for how to confirm properly.

## Setting boundaries with parents

You are the expert in the room, and most parents respect that once you claim it politely.

- **If a parent micromanages lessons**, propose running the sessions your way and giving a detailed debrief after each one. This keeps them informed without directing the teaching.
- **If messages arrive late at night**, reply the next morning. You do not need to be on call, and a calm reply at a reasonable hour models good boundaries.
- **If a parent is rude or argues about billing**, keep your replies brief and objective, state the facts, and loop in MatchMax Admin so there is a record. Never get drawn into a long back-and-forth in the chat.
