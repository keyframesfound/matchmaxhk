Three steps:

1. **Apply.** Use the **Apply as a Partner Centre** button on the [Centres & Educators guide](/guide/partners), or contact the partnerships team directly.
2. **Verify.** Send your organisation details and business registration. We verify the entity before any listing goes live.
3. **Sign & launch.** After verification you complete the B2B Partner Agreement, and your **Centre Workspace** is linked to your admin account: publish your centre profile and courses, then start receiving qualified enquiries.

Enquiries arrive with the essentials filled in: parent vs independent student, curriculum, and goals, so your team can reply with context instead of questions.
