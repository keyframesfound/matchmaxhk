**Independent Tutor = solo self-employed.** One person, one listing, one admin fee per private case. Independent tutors on MatchMax are self-employed operators under Cap. 57, never MatchMax employees or agents.

**Centre / Organisation = a registered business workspace.** Tuition centres, academies, and consultancies run multiple staff under one business workspace: enquiries, listings, and referrals are managed from a shared Centre Workspace Portal without exposing personal tutor profiles.

In practice, choose a centre account when more than one person needs to work the pipeline, when enquiries should reach a desk rather than an individual, or when you publish courses under a registered brand.
