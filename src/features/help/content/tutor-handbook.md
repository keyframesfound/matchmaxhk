The MatchMax Tutor Handbook is the practical playbook for reliable operations, confident communication, and elite teaching. It is written for fast skimming and immediate lesson application — no dense academic jargon.

## Why a handbook?

Parents booking through MatchMax aren't just looking for someone who knows the syllabus; they are paying for peace of mind, reliability, and an elite service experience. Every tutor represents MatchMax and shapes our reputation — the handbook exists so you can look like a seasoned professional from lesson one.

For the conversational side — parent-relations scripts and etiquette protocols — the [Tutor Playbook](/tutor-playbook) remains the quick reference.
