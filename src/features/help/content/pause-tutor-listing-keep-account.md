Yes. In **Settings**, set your tutor listing to **Paused**. Your profile stops appearing in search and you stop receiving new case invitations, but nothing is deleted.

While paused:

- Your **Learning Mode** account keeps working normally: book lessons for yourself or your children, buy materials, and manage bookings.
- Your **verified hours** and reviews stay on your record.
- Existing confirmed lessons run to completion as agreed.

When you are ready to take cases again, switch the listing back on from the same place. Pausing is the right move between exam seasons, during a busy work period, or any time you want to learn without teaching.
