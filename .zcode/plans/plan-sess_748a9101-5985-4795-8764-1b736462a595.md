# Airbnb-style hamburger menu

## Goal
Restyle the desktop hamburger popover to Airbnb's menu structure from your screenshot: sectioned icon rows, the "Become a Tutor" promo card, a plain-text extras section, and Log out at the bottom. The avatar button becomes a direct link (no dropdown) — the burger holds everything.

## 1. Burger menu content — full Airbnb mapping (`SiteHeader.tsx`)

Width `w-[20rem]` (320px), `rounded-2xl`, border, flat (no shadows), full-bleed hairline dividers between sections, rows `text-[15px]` with `h-5` icons and grey rounded hover wash.

**Logged-in:**
- **Section A — account rows (icons):** Saved Posts → /saved-posts · My Business → /business (if org) · Profile → /dashboard?hash=profile · Settings → /dashboard · Admin → /admin (if admin)
- **Section B — preferences:** Language row (Globe + trailing "EN"/"繁", `i18n.changeLanguage`) · Dark/Light mode row (Moon/Sun) · Help Center → /help
- **Section C — promo card → /join:** bold "Become a Tutor" + subtitle "It's easy to start tutoring and earn extra income." with `nav-become-tutor.png` at ~48px on the right ( Airbnb's "Become a host" card)
- **Section D — plain text (no icons):** Post a case → /case-request · For Business → /pricing (flag) · Courses → /courses (flag)
- **Bottom:** divider + Log out

**Logged-out:** Section A becomes bold "Sign up" / "Log in" rows → /auth; Sections B–D identical; no Log out.

## 2. Avatar button becomes a link
- Logged-in: `Link` to `/dashboard?hash=profile` (same initial circle, hover wash, focus ring).
- Logged-out: `Link` to `/auth`.
- Delete the avatar `DropdownMenu` and its content entirely (Profile/Settings/Saved/Business/Admin/Sign out now live only in the burger — every item appears once).
- "Become a Tutor" stays as the header text link too (Airbnb shows "Become a host" in both places).

## 3. i18n (both locales)
