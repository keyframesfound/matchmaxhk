## Remove minimum-score checks from the tutor application

**Goal:** Applicants must still fill in the Overall Score field, but the score-level gates are removed — an IBDP applicant with 38 is no longer rejected, and none of the per-curriculum score ranges are enforced anymore. Marketing copy stays untouched.

### The change (one file: `src/lib/tutor-application.schema.ts`)

Delete the seven per-curriculum `overallScore` validation blocks inside the schema's `superRefine` (lines 274–348):

- IBDP — currently requires 40–45 (the real quality floor)
- HKDSE — numeric Best 5 check
- SAT — 400–1600
- IELTS — band 4.0–9.0 regex
- ISAT — 100–200
- UCAT(3600) — 1200–3600 in 10-point steps
- UCAT(2700) — 900–2700 in 10-point steps

After deletion, `overallScore` stays `min(1, "Required").max(200)` — required free-form for every curriculum, exactly how A-Level / IGCSE / AP already behave today. The `isProfessional` flag and all other checks in `superRefine` (student card, university, start date, portfolio consent, etc.) stay.

Because both the client submit (`safeParse` in `ApplicationForm.tsx`) and the server handler (`submitTutorApplication` re-parses with the same schema) validate through this one schema, deleting the blocks removes the gates at both ends with no other code changes.

### Explicitly left unchanged (per your answers)

- **Score fields stay required** — Overall Score, Best 6, and Subject Scores rows keep their current "Required" step gates (steps 2 and 3 of the academic path).
- **Inputs untouched** — number input types, placeholders, grade dropdowns, and the AI transcript auto-fill all stay as-is.
- **Marketing copy stays** — the /how-it-works academic-standards table and professional-tier blurb, and the professional-pathway notice sentence in the form ("minimum overall achievement of 30 in Best 5") all remain. Flagging the last one since it states a floor in form copy — say the word if you'd rather drop that sentence too.
- **Admin unchanged** — join-requests already renders `overallScore || "—"`; historical applications keep showing their scores. No DB migration needed (payload is a jsonb blob).
